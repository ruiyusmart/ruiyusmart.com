What we do

Three disciplines. One engineering bench.

From the silicon on the bench to the screen in your hand, Raywise builds the parts of an industrial system that other suppliers have to glue together.

Smart Control

PLC, SCADA, edge controllers — engineered for industrial-grade reliability and built to outlast the cycle.

Industrial Automation

Equipment, sensors, and the wiring between them. Spec, source, install, and commission from one team.

IoT & Software

Connected devices, firmware, cloud, and the apps that put it on a phone. From sensor to screen.
