Nine business lines · One engineering bench

Hardware, software, and the apps that tie them together.

Raywise is an integrated house — we design the controllers, source the components, write the firmware, build the cloud, and ship the mobile app. The nine business lines below are how we package that capability for our customers.

Every project starts with the same conversation: what are you trying to control, and who needs to see it? The rest is engineering, sourcing, and shipping. Browse the nine lines below — each one is a real team at Raywise, with a real track record, and a real mobile app on the App Store and Google Play.

01

Smart Control Systems

PLC, SCADA, edge controllers, and HMI panels — engineered for industrial-grade reliability. We design control logic, commission on site, and stand behind it for the life of the line.

  • PLC programming and retrofits (Siemens, Allen-Bradley, Mitsubishi)
  • SCADA dashboards, alarm rationalisation, historian integration
  • Edge controllers with secure remote access
  • Functional safety reviews and CE / UL documentation
Discuss a control project
02

Industrial Automation Products

From a single motor starter to a full robotic cell — sourced, integrated, and commissioned. We carry the parts and we know the people who make them.

  • Variable-frequency drives, servo systems, soft starters
  • Robotic cells (pick-and-place, palletising, machine tending)
  • Vision inspection and quality-control loops
  • Pneumatic, hydraulic, and electromechanical subsystems
Talk to an integrator
03

IoT Device R&D & Technical Services

Connected devices, edge firmware, and the cloud they talk to. We do the schematic, the layout, the enclosure, the MQTT bridge, and the OTA update pipeline.

  • Hardware design (schematic, PCB layout, EMC-aware enclosure)
  • Embedded firmware (C / C++ / Rust, FreeRTOS, Zephyr)
  • Telemetry pipelines (MQTT, HTTPS, LoRaWAN, NB-IoT)
  • OTA update infrastructure and fleet management
Brief us on your device

  • Web apps (React, Vue, server-rendered)
  • Mobile apps (native iOS, native Android, cross-platform)
  • Back-end services and APIs (Node, Go, Python)
  • DevOps, CI/CD, observability, and SLOs

Import & Export of Goods and Technology
